"study in usa from nepal": IDP Nepal (UK, USA) and KIEC (UK).

guide to *which sections exist*, not as a source of figures. Every number we publish must be

before it goes on the page. Do not copy competitor figures.

## UK page: missing sections

1. Eligibility and admission requirements (+2 percentage, bachelor's grade, IELTS by level)
2. Document checklist for the student visa (CAS, finance proof, TB test)
3. Named scholarships (university awards, Chevening, Commonwealth)
4. Work rights during study and the Graduate Route after study
5. Top universities table (name, QS rank, notes), not only logos
6. Popular courses (UG and PG)
7. Itemised cost of living (accommodation, food, transport, utilities), plus visa fee in NPR
8. Pre-departure guide and student success stories (KIEC has both)
9. FAQ gaps: gap year accepted, best time to apply, can I change course, scholarship
   coverage, how many intakes

## USA page: missing sections

1. NPR equivalents anywhere (tuition, total cost, visa fee)
2. Named university list with rank and fee range, not only logos
3. Job prospects and post-study work (OPT/CPT currently only in the FAQ)
4. Itemised cost of living (rent, food, books, insurance)
5. Tuition for community college and doctoral study (we only list UG, PG, MBA)
6. Admission rules: minimum percentage/GPA and how many gap years are accepted
7. Visa types (F-1, J-1, M-1) and when each applies
8. More scholarships (#YouAreWelcomeHere, AAUW) beyond Fulbright and Humphrey
9. FAQ gaps: exams needed, gap acceptance, percentage required
