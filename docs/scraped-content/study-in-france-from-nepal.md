## France Student Visa Requirements for Nepalese Students

Nepalese students pursuing studies longer than 3 months require a Long-Stay Student Visa (VLS-TS).

### Required Documents Table:

| # | Document | Details |
|---|----------|---------|
| 1 | Valid Passport | At least 3 months beyond stay |
| 2 | Campus France Registration | Mandatory for visa processing |
| 3 | University Acceptance Letter | From recognized French institution |
| 4 | Proof of Accommodation | Booking confirmation or housing letter |
| 5 | Financial Proof | €615/month via bank statement or sponsor |
| 6 | Insurance | Medical & travel coverage up to €30,000 |
| 7 | Academic Documents | Transcripts, certificates, CV, SOP |
| 8 | Passport-Size Photos | Recent, per visa guidelines |
| 9 | Visa Fee Receipt | Approx. €50–€99 |
| 10 | Visa Application Form | Available at france-visas.gouv.fr |

**Note:** Visa application submission occurs at VFS Global Kathmandu following Campus France approval.

## Cost of Studying in France from Nepal

France ranks among Europe's most economical study destinations. Public institution tuition receives government subsidization, significantly lowering costs compared to alternative countries. Students additionally benefit from affordable healthcare and housing assistance programs.

### Estimated Annual Expenses for Nepalese Students:

| Category | Approximate Cost |
|----------|------------------|
| Public University Tuition | €170 – €600/year (UG), €250 – €700 (PG) |
| Private Institutions | €3,000 – €10,000/year |
| Living Expenses | €8,000 – €12,000/year |
| Accommodation (Monthly) | €200 – €600 depending on city |
| Health Insurance | Around €300/year |
| Transportation & Misc. | €50 – €100/month |

**Tip for Nepalese Students:** CAF (French Housing Assistance) may reduce monthly rent by up to 35%.

## FAQs (Frequently Asked Questions)

**Q: Can I study in France without knowing French?**
A: Yes. Many universities offer full-time programs in English, particularly at the postgraduate level.

**Q: Do I need IELTS to study in France from Nepal?**
A: Yes, most English-taught programs require IELTS or equivalent test.

**Q: How long can I stay in France after graduation?**
A: Students may apply for post-study work visas valid for up to 2 years after graduation.

**Q: What is the average cost of studying in France?**
A: Public university tuition runs approximately €2,770–€3,770 annually. Living expenses span €700–€1,200 monthly.

**Q: Are scholarships available for Nepalese students?**
A: Yes. Charpak, Eiffel, and Erasmus+ scholarships accept Nepalese applicants.

**Q: Can I work part-time while studying?**
A: Yes. International students may work up to 964 hours annually (averaging 20 hours weekly).

**Q: When should I start the application process?**
A: Begin 6–9 months before intended intake for admissions and visa processing.

**Q: Does Admizz help with French student visa applications?**
A: Absolutely. Admizz provides complete documentation and guidance for France visa applications.
