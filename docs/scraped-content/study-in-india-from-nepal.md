## Indian Visa Requirements Section

**Key Point:** "Nepalese students do not require a traditional student visa to study in India due to the open-border policy."

However, registration at the university and FRRO documentation may be required depending on duration and region.

**Visa Requirements Table:**
- Visa Needed: No visa required for Nepalese nationals
- Entry: Valid passport or citizenship card
- University Registration: Required at time of admission
- FRRO/Police Registration: Not required except some cases based on duration/region
- Study Permit: Academic documents and NOC from Nepal's Ministry of Education
