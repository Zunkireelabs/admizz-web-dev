## South Korea Student Visa for Nepalese Students

**Visa Type:** D-2 (Student Visa)

**Issued For:** Bachelor's, Master's, and Doctoral degree programs

**Where to Apply:** Embassy of the Republic of Korea, Kathmandu

**Processing Time:** "2 to 4 weeks (after submission of all required documents)"

**Validity:** Usually matches the duration of your study program

**Work Rights:** "Up to 20 hours per week during semesters; full-time during holidays (with permission)"

## Korea University Intakes for Nepalese Students

Two main academic intakes exist:

**Spring Intake:** March - described as the "Main intake—ideal for new academic year start in most universities"

**Fall Intake:** September - noted as a "Secondary intake—fewer programs, but still offers major course options"

The page notes most Nepalese students apply for Fall intake to allow time for scholarships and visa procedures.

## FAQs (Frequently Asked Questions)

**Q: Can I apply after completing +2 in Nepal?**
A: "Yes, you can apply for undergraduate programs in Korea after completing Grade 12 from NEB, CBSE, or other equivalent boards. Some universities may require you to meet GPA and language proficiency criteria"

**Q: Is Korean language (TOPIK) mandatory to study in South Korea?**
A: "Not always. For English-taught programs, Korean language is not compulsory. However, for Korean-taught courses or to apply for government scholarships like GKS, a TOPIK Level 3 or higher may be required."

**Q: Can Nepalese students work part-time while studying in Korea?**
A: "Yes. International students with a D-2 visa can apply for part-time work permits after completing 6 months of study. You can work up to 20 hours/week during semesters and unlimited hours during holidays"

**Q: Are scholarships available for Nepalese students in South Korea?**
A: "Yes. Korea offers many scholarships for Nepalese students, such as the GKS (Global Korea Scholarship), university-based merit scholarships, and research fellowships. These may cover tuition fees, living expenses, and airfare."

**Q: What is the student visa type for South Korea and how do I apply?**
A: You need the "D-2 student visa, which allows full-time study at Korean universities. You'll need a university acceptance letter, academic documents, financial proof, and a visa application form."

**Q: What English proficiency tests are accepted by Korean universities?**
A: "Many Korean universities accept IELTS, TOEFL, or Duolingo English Test for English-taught programs. For Korean-medium programs, English test scores may not be required, but Korean proficiency (TOPIK) is essential."

**Q: When is the best time to apply for Korean university intakes?**
A: Korean intakes are "Spring Intake: March" and "Fall Intake: September. You should start preparing and applying at least 6–9 months in advance"

**Q: Does Admizz provide Korean language or entrance exam preparation?**
A: "Yes. Admizz offers basic Korean language support, guidance for TOPIK, and preparation counseling for university-specific entrance tests (if applicable). Our team also helps with interview training and document translation"
