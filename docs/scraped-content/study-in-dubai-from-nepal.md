## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Why should I choose Dubai for higher education?**
A: "Dubai offers globally recognized degrees, multicultural exposure, modern infrastructure, affordable tuition fees, and excellent job prospects after graduation."

**Q: Are degrees from Dubai universities valid internationally?**
A: "Yes, most Dubai universities are affiliated with or accredited by UK, US, or Australian institutions, making their degrees widely accepted worldwide."

**Q: What are the major intakes for Dubai universities?**
A: "The primary intakes are September (Fall) and January/February (Spring). Some universities also offer a May/July (Summer) intake."

**Q: Do I need IELTS or TOEFL to apply to Dubai universities?**
A: "Yes, most universities require English proficiency tests like IELTS, TOEFL, PTE, or Duolingo. Some may offer in-house placement tests or waive it with an English-medium education letter."

**Q: How much does it cost to study in Dubai from Nepal?**
A: "Tuition ranges from AED 30,000 to AED 100,000 per year, and living expenses can range between AED 2,500 – 5,000 per month."

**Q: Are there scholarships available for Nepalese students?**
A: "Yes, many universities offer merit-based, need-based, and sports/talent scholarships to Nepalese students. Some also provide early-bird discounts and sibling/alumni waivers."

**Q: Can I work part-time while studying in Dubai?**
A: "Yes, students can work part-time (up to 15–20 hours/week) during semesters and full-time during breaks, provided they have a work permit through the university."

**Q: Is a student visa required to study in Dubai?**
A: "Yes, students need a UAE residence visa for education, usually sponsored by the university. Visa processing takes about 2–4 weeks."

**Q: What documents are required for admission?**
A: "Academic transcripts, Passport copy, Passport-size photos, English test score (IELTS/TOEFL), Statement of Purpose (SOP), Recommendation letters (if required)"

**Q: How can Admizz Education help me?**
A: "Admizz Education provides free guidance on university selection, test prep, scholarships, application filing, visa support, and pre-departure briefings."
