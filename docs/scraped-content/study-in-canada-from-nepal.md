## Student Visa Requirements for Canada (Nepalese Students)

To study in Canada, students must secure a study permit. **Key Requirements:**
- Letter of Acceptance from a Canadian institution
- Proof of sufficient funds
- Valid passport
- SOP (Statement of Purpose)
- Visa application and biometrics
- Medical and police clearance certificates

## FAQs Related to Study in Canada from Nepal

**Q: Can Nepalese students work while studying in Canada?**
A: Yes, up to 20 hours per week during semesters and full-time during breaks.

**Q: Is IELTS compulsory for studying in Canada?**
A: Most institutions require IELTS, TOEFL, PTE, or Duolingo. Some offer pathway programs without IELTS.

**Q: What is the cost of studying in Canada?**
A: Tuition fees range from CAD 15,000 to CAD 30,000/year depending on the course.

**Q: How long does it take to process a Canada student visa?**
A: Generally 4–8 weeks, depending on your application and biometrics.

**Q: Can studying in Canada lead to PR?**
A: Yes, through PGWP and Canadian work experience, students can apply for PR.

**Q: What scholarships are available for Nepalese students?**
A: Vanier, OGS, Lester B. Pearson, McGill, and other university-specific awards.

**Q: Do Nepalese students need proof of funds?**
A: Yes, sufficient funds must be shown to cover tuition and living costs.

**Q: Which are the most popular courses in Canada for Nepalese students?**
A: Business, IT, Engineering, Data Science, Nursing, and Social Sciences.

**Q: Can students apply to Canadian colleges instead of universities?**
A: Yes, colleges are affordable and offer industry-focused programs.

**Q: How can Admizz Education help me?**
